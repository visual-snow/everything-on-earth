# Skills Lab VR Nursing Simulator Capabilities

Skills Lab is an HTC Vive VR training simulation built in Unity3D for nursing students. Students practice the full medication administration workflow: retrieving medications from a VANAS shelf, selecting appropriate needle and syringe sizes, verifying patient identity, choosing the injection site, performing the injection, and disposing of sharps. The project was last active in 2018.

## Patient Scenarios

- Four virtual patient scenarios cover different clinical contexts: adult man, elder man, young adult, and pregnant woman
- Each scenario presents a medication order that the student must fulfil through the complete workflow

## Medication Handling

- A simulated VANAS shelf holds medications that students must locate and retrieve
- An external medicine database website provides reference information
- Needle gauge selection ranges from 18G to 27G
- Syringe volume selection ranges from 1.5 ml to 50 ml

## Injection Procedure

- Students select the injection method: subcutaneous, intravenous, or intramuscular
- Injection site interaction is performed on the virtual patient
- Sharps disposal completes the workflow

## Constraints

- Requires HTC Vive hardware; no other VR headset is documented as supported
- Built in Unity3D with 3D assets from Cinema4D and Blender; last active in 2018
- No scoring, assessment rubric, or performance feedback mechanism
- No multiplayer mode or instructor observation dashboard
- No LMS integration or SCORM export
- No physics-based fluid or dose simulation
