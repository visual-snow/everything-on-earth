Trauma Quick Reference

Tension Pneumothorax

Tracheal deviation, absent breath sounds, JVD, hypotension. Needle decompression: 2nd intercostal space, midclavicular line.

IMMEDIATE

Cardiac Tamponade

Beck's triad: hypotension, muffled heart sounds, JVD. Pericardiocentesis or thoracotomy required.

IMMEDIATE

Splenic Rupture

Left upper quadrant pain, Kehr's sign (referred left shoulder pain). FAST exam for free fluid. Splenectomy may be needed.

IMMEDIATE

Crush Syndrome

After prolonged compression (>1hr). Dark urine = myoglobinuria. Hyperkalemia risk = cardiac arrest. Aggressive IV fluids BEFORE release. Tourniquet if potassium uncontrolled.

IMMEDIATE

Traumatic Brain Injury

GCS assessment: Eye (4) + Verbal (5) + Motor (6) = 15. GCS <8 = severe, intubate. Cushing's triad: hypertension, bradycardia, irregular breathing = raised ICP.

IMMEDIATE

Cervical Spine Injury

Immobilize. Log roll only. Do NOT remove impaled objects. Assess motor/sensory in all 4 limbs. Priapism + flaccid paralysis = spinal shock.

HIGH PRIORITY

Femoral Artery Hemorrhage

Inguinal region bleed. Tourniquet high and tight. Junctional hemorrhage (groin/axilla/neck) needs direct pressure + hemostatic agents.

IMMEDIATE

Blast Lung Injury

Primary blast wave. Hemoptysis, dyspnea, chest tightness. CXR: bilateral "butterfly" infiltrates. Avoid positive pressure ventilation if possible (risk of air embolism).

HIGH PRIORITY

Tourniquet Application

2-3 inches above wound, NOT on joint. Mark time applied on forehead. Do NOT loosen in field. Effective time: up to 6 hours for limb survival.

PROCEDURE

FAST Exam Points

Focused Assessment with Sonography

4 views: (1) RUQ - Morrison's pouch (2) LUQ - splenorenal (3) Suprapubic - bladder (4) Subxiphoid - pericardium. Free fluid = surgical abdomen.
