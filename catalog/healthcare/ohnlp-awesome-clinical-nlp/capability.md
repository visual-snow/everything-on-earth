# Awesome Clinical NLP Capabilities

Awesome Clinical NLP is a curated reference list of clinical natural language processing resources maintained by the OHNLP group. It catalogues NLP systems, EHR text-search tools, manual annotation platforms, survey articles, COVID-19 NLP resources, and workshop/shared-task links. It contains no runnable code, model weights, or deployable components.

## Coverage

- NLP systems section lists major clinical NLP tools including cTAKES, CLAMP, MedTagger, MetaMap, and the CD2H NLP Sandbox
- EHR text-search systems section covers tools for searching clinical text within electronic health record systems
- Manual annotation tools section references Brat, Anafora, MAE, and related labeling platforms
- Overview articles section links to methodology reviews and survey papers on clinical NLP
- COVID-19 NLP resources section collects pandemic-specific NLP tools and datasets
- Workshop and shared-task resources section covers BioNLP, ClinicalNLP, and n2c2 community events

## Constraints

- Read-only reference list; contains no runnable code, libraries, or deployable components
- Last updated in October 2020; external links may be stale or removed
- Does not cover post-2020 advances including large language models, clinical foundation models, or instruction-tuned clinical assistants
- Scope is limited to clinical NLP; a sister list is recommended for broader biomedical information extraction
- No hosted model weights, evaluation datasets, benchmarks, or leaderboards
